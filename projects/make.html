Make / AI Document Automation

Attachment Processor

An AI-assisted document-processing workflow that detects email attachments, processes each attachment individually, stores files in Google Drive, and records structured information in Google Sheets.

DOCUMENTS,

02 / Attachment Processing

EXTRACT EACH
ATTACHMENT.

Gmail detects the incoming message and lists its attachments. An iterator then separates the files so every attachment can move through the remaining workflow individually and reliably.

  • Detect incoming Gmail attachments
  • List all files attached to the message
  • Iterate through each attachment separately

03 / AI Processing

TURN FILES
INTO DATA.

AI processes each attachment and extracts structured information that can be passed consistently to the storage and logging steps of the scenario.

  • Process attachments one at a time
  • Extract useful structured information
  • Prepare consistent records for downstream tools

04 / Storage & Logging

STORE, TRACK,
REVIEW.

Processed files are stored in Google Drive while the extracted data is logged in Google Sheets for clear tracking and review.
